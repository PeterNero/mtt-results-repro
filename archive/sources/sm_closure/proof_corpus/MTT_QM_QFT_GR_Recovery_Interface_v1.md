# MTT QM/QFT/GR Recovery Interface v1

## Purpose

This artifact defines the recovery boundary for QM, local QFT, GR, and unit
anchors at the SM-parity standard.

It does not derive Born weights, QFT locality, Einstein dynamics, or physical
absolute normalization.  It declares the interfaces MTT must expose before
empirical equivalence can be audited.

## Recovery Interfaces

- `QM`: required maps: state carrier H_S or modal-state object; observable algebra Obs_QM(S); Born/record probability rule or measured parity replacement; projection/update or record-conditioning map. Parity: MTT may declare the standard QM Hilbert/observable/measurement interface as parity structure, with Born/record derivation left as a no-knob target. Forbidden: Do not use agreement with a measured quantum outcome to choose the sector source. No-knob target: derive Born weights and record selection from admissibility/coherence/fixed-point structure.
- `QFT`: required maps: local field/operator algebra A(O); gauge action and covariant derivative; renormalization scale and scheme map; S-matrix/correlator/observable extraction map. Parity: MTT may recover local QFT by an explicit local observable functor and measured renormalized parameter slots. Forbidden: Do not treat benchmark correlators or fitted thresholds as selected operator data. No-knob target: derive local QFT functor and threshold kernels from selected modal/operator packets.
- `GR`: required maps: metric or effective metric map g(S); connection/curvature construction; stress-energy coupling interface; Bianchi/conservation consistency condition; dimensionful normalization slot. Parity: MTT may use the standard GR metric/stress-energy interface with measured dimensionful anchors for parity. Forbidden: Do not claim physical absolute normalization from an internal dimensionless identity. No-knob target: derive Newton scale, cosmological scale, and metric dynamics from selected modal gap/source data.
- `Units`: required maps: unit convention registry; dimensionful anchor slots; conversion and uncertainty propagation map. Parity: MTT may declare unit conventions and measured anchors at parity standard. Forbidden: Do not hide a physical scale choice inside a unit convention. No-knob target: derive physical absolute scale without measured dimensional anchor.

## Recovery Rules

- Recovery interfaces are structural maps, not numerical fits.
- Each recovered sector must declare its carriers, observables, parameter slots, and conventions.
- Measured values may enter only after the relevant recovery map and sector boundary are declared.
- QM recovery must distinguish measurement axioms admitted for parity from no-knob Born/record derivation.
- QFT recovery must distinguish selected local operator structure from measured running parameters.
- GR recovery must distinguish metric/stress-energy structure from measured dimensionful normalization.
- Unit conventions must be explicit and cannot carry hidden physical content.

## Cross-Sector Consistency

- `qm_to_qft`: QFT states and correlators must reduce to the declared QM state/observable interface where applicable.
- `qft_to_sm`: The SM sector embedding must be a special case of the local QFT recovery interface.
- `qft_to_gr`: Stress-energy generated by QFT/SM observables must be compatible with the GR coupling interface.
- `gr_to_units`: Dimensionful GR quantities must pass through the unit/anchor registry.
- `measurement_to_empirical_ledger`: Predicted or measured observables must be recorded with provenance, uncertainty, and convention.

## Forbidden Shortcuts

- using empirical agreement to infer the recovery map after the fact
- treating Born rule, locality, or Einstein dynamics as no-knob derived without an internal proof
- using measured Newton scale as a derived physical normalization
- using fitted QFT thresholds as selected operator spectra
- using unit conventions as hidden dimensionful constants
- claiming full SM-parity closure before empirical equivalence is audited

## Acceptance Tests

- QM, QFT, GR, and Units interfaces are present.
- Each interface lists required maps, parity acceptance, forbidden shortcut, and no-knob target.
- Cross-sector consistency checks are declared.
- Measured parameters remain downstream of recovery maps.
- No no-knob derivation is claimed for Born weights, QFT thresholds, or physical Newton scale.
- Full SM-parity closure remains open until empirical equivalence is audited.

## Interface Theorem

MTT reaches the recovery-interface stage of SM-parity when it declares QM,
QFT, GR, and unit-anchor maps with explicit carriers, observables, parameter
slots, and conventions, and when all measured values are downstream of those
maps.

This permits comparison with accepted QM/QFT/GR practice at parity standard,
while preserving the no-knob targets as open derivation obligations.

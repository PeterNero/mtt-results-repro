# Notation and Conventions

This document defines the essential mathematical notation used throughout the papers.
It is intended for LLM interpretation and omits numbering, cross-references, and LaTeX infrastructure.

## Spaces
- $\mathcal H$ — ambient Hilbert space.
- $\mathcal H_{\mathrm{coh}}$ — coherent subspace of $\mathcal H$.
- $\mathcal H_{\mathrm{inc}}$ — incoherent (complementary) subspace of $\mathcal H$.

## Operators and maps
- $L$ — linearized operator governing the dynamics.
- $Q$ — projector onto the incoherent subspace $\mathcal H_{\mathrm{inc}}$.
- $P$ — projector onto the coherent subspace $\mathcal H_{\mathrm{coh}}$.
- $\operatorname{Ran}(Q)$ — range of the projector $Q$.
- $F$ — nonlinear vector field defining the evolution equation.
- $\Phi_t$ — flow map generated by $F$ at time $t$.

## Distinguished states
- $\Psi_\ast$ — equilibrium or reference state about which the system is linearized.

## Norms and pairings
- $\langle x,y\rangle$ — inner product on $\mathcal H$.
- $\|x\|$ — norm induced by the inner product on $\mathcal H$.

## Semigroup notation
- $e^{tL}$ — semigroup generated by the operator $L$.
- $e^{tL}Q$ — restriction of the semigroup to the incoherent sector.

## Constants and rates
- $\lambda_Q$ — exponential decay rate associated with the incoherent dynamics.
- $\mu$ — positive constant appearing in dissipativity or monotonicity estimates.

## General conventions
- All operators act on $\mathcal H$ unless stated otherwise.
- All estimates are uniform in time unless explicitly restricted.

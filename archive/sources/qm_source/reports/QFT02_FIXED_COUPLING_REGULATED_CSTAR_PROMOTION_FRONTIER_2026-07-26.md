## Result

The first normed step is now closed:

- every finite auxiliary external regulator carrying the declared q79
  compact gauge representation has a finite physical Cstar algebra;
- compact-gauge Haar reduction and the closed-region Gauss-neutral corner
  are exact;

   positivity and weighted cutoff convergence uniquely sums the certified

## Most Valuable Next Calculation

The best next target is the tangent-and-uniformity package for one explicit
q79 regulator family:

1. select one external gauge-covariant regulator from the upper action;
2. differentiate its finite-coupling local observables at zero coupling;
3. compare those derivatives with the certified Lorentzian EG/SP jet;
4. prove a regulator-independent Lieb-Robinson or asymptotic
   microcausality estimate;
5. prove a compatible norm-level Ward estimate.

If the tangent comparison fails, the regulator is not the q79 theory. If it
passes but uniform estimates fail, it remains a controlled effective
regulator rather than a continuum completion.

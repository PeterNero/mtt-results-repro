# MTT Selected Renormalized-SM Observable Functor From Common-Scheme Action v1

The local-QFT frontier is closed at the adopted profile/parity standard by the
composition

```text
Obs_SM^MTT = Readout o LSZ o Green o Q_SM o E_SM.
```

`E_SM` sends the selected MTT SM branch to the gauge-fixed renormalized
Standard Model action at the selected SMDR MSbar parameter point. `Q_SM` is the
standard BRST/Faddeev-Popov perturbative quantization. Functional
differentiation and Legendre transform give Green functions and 1PI vertices;
LSZ gives amplitudes; the declared readout gives infrared-safe observables.

Because the embedded action, measure convention, scheme, scale, and parameter
point are identical, the generating functionals are identical on this branch.
All perturbative correlators, amplitudes, and inclusive observables are therefore
equal order by order. A finite table of example decays is useful validation but
is not a mathematical substitute for, or prerequisite of, this functor theorem.

This imports standard SM quantization as parity structure. It does not derive
BRST quantization, the path-integral measure, or Born/record rules from MTT and
therefore does not close strict no-knob or constructive nonperturbative QFT.

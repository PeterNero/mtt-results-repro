theory obligation. CKM global-fit central values and uncertainties change with

likelihood or declared uncertainty-profile comparison.

[CKMfitter Summer 2025](https://ckmfitter.in2p3.fr/www/results/plots_summer25/ckm_res_summer25.html),
and [UTfit Summer 2025](https://www.utfit.org/foswiki/bin/view/UTfit/ResultsSummer2025SM)
report CKM inference.

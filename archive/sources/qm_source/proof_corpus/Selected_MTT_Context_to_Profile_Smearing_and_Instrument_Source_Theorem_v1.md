# Selected MTT Context-to-Profile, Smearing and Instrument Source Theorem

## Scope

This theorem answers a precise conditional question:

```text
If one selected MTT action supplies a physical preparation/apparatus context,
which additional choices are needed to construct

    C -> (Z_C, f_C, ell_hat_C, J_C, instrument_C)?
```

The answer is: none after the action supplies the typed source package below.
The construction is canonical, covariant and finite-exact. The present MTT
corpus has not yet emitted the complete physical package, so this is a source
reduction theorem rather than closure of `B.QM.01`.

## 1. Admissible Same-Source Package

For a physical context `C`, let the selected MTT action and boundary data emit:

1. finite detected input and joint system-apparatus output Hilbert spaces
   `K_C` and `H_C`;
2. a differentiable action-generated evolve-project map `F_C` and its
   derivative
   \[
   D_C=dF_C:K_C\longrightarrow H_C;
   \]
3. an atomic stable apparatus record algebra `R_C` whose two minimal central
   projections are `Pi_C,+` and `Pi_C,-`;
4. two retained branch configurations `phi_C,+` and `phi_C,-` of the same
   matter action;
5. an external spatial density operator
   \(\widehat\Delta_C\geq0\), obtained from the spatial Hessian/Hodge block of
   that action;
6. its selected finite spectral projector
   \[
   P_C^{\rm sp}
   =\mathbf 1_{[0,\Lambda_C]}(\widehat\Delta_C),
   \]
   and reduced Green operator
   \[
   \widehat G_C
   =Q_{0,C}
    (\widehat\Delta_C|_{\operatorname{Ran}Q_{0,C}})^{-1}Q_{0,C},
   \qquad
   Q_{0,C}=I-P_{\ker\widehat\Delta_C}.
   \]

The following same-source identities are mandatory:

- `F_C` is the evolution generated by the selected action, not a fitted
  detector map;
- `R_C` is stable under that same joint evolution;
- \(\widehat\Delta_C\) is the external spatial density Hessian of that action;
- \(P_C^{\rm sp}\) and \(\widehat G_C\) are spectral functions of
  \(\widehat\Delta_C\);
- both branch stress tensors are metric variations of the same matter action.

Assume `D_C` is injective on `K_C`. Set
\[
G_C=D_C^\ast D_C,
\qquad
U_C=D_CG_C^{-1/2}.
\]
Then \(U_C^\ast U_C=I_{K_C}\).

For the binary involution below, impose the projective-context condition
\[
E_{C,\pm}:=U_C^\ast\Pi_{C,\pm}U_C,
\qquad
E_{C,\pm}^2=E_{C,\pm},
\qquad
E_{C,+}+E_{C,-}=I.
\]
This condition is not automatic for a general POVM. If it fails, the context
still has an instrument, but it does not have a canonical binary involution
`Z_C`.

## 2. The Five-Output Functor

### 2.1 Context involution

Define
\[
Z_C=E_{C,+}-E_{C,-}
   =U_C^\ast(\Pi_{C,+}-\Pi_{C,-})U_C.
\]
Then \(Z_C^\ast=Z_C\) and \(Z_C^2=I\).

### 2.2 Variational mass profile

\[

=-\frac{2}{\sqrt{|g|}}
  \frac{\delta S_{\rm matter}[\phi,g]}{\delta g^{\mu\nu}},

\[
\delta\mu_C
=n^\mu n^\nu

Thus the branch-to-profile rule is not an independent phenomenological map:
it is metric variation followed by the external spatial projection emitted by
the same action.

### 2.3 Resolution

Let \(\Lambda_C>0\) be the largest positive eigenvalue retained by
\(P_C^{\rm sp}\). Define
\[
\widehat\ell_C=\Lambda_C^{-1/2}.
\]

The exact regulator is the spectral projector \(P_C^{\rm sp}\), not an
independently chosen Gaussian. The number \(\widehat\ell_C\) records its

smearing length is a separate model-matching statement.

### 2.4 Newton functional

Define
\[
\mathcal J_C
=4\pi\langle f_C,\widehat G_C f_C\rangle.
\]
If
\(\widehat\Delta_Cu_j=\lambda_ju_j\), this is the exact finite sum
\[
\mathcal J_C
=4\pi
 \sum_{\substack{0<\lambda_j\leq\Lambda_C}}
 \frac{|\langle f_C,u_j\rangle|^2}{\lambda_j}.
\]

On flat external three-space with
\(-\Delta(4\pi|x-y|)^{-1}=\delta(x-y)\), this agrees with
\[
\mathcal J_C
=\int\!\!\int
 \frac{f_C(\xi)f_C(\eta)}{|\xi-\eta|}
 \,d^3\xi\,d^3\eta

on the declared infrared domain. Compact spaces require the reduced Green
operator and the corresponding zero-mode convention.

### 2.5 Instrument

Define
\[
M_{C,\pm}
=\Pi_{C,\pm}D_CG_C^{-1/2}
=\Pi_{C,\pm}U_C
\]
and
\[
\mathcal I_{C,\pm}(\rho)
=M_{C,\pm}\rho M_{C,\pm}^\ast.

Then
\[
\sum_{\pm}M_{C,\pm}^\ast M_{C,\pm}=I,
\]
so \(\{\mathcal I_{C,+},\mathcal I_{C,-}\}\) is a normalized quantum
instrument from the detected input to the joint output.

This resolves the Kraus-unravelling ambiguity only when the physical record
algebra and its central projectors are selected by the action. A nonselective
channel by itself cannot do so.

## 3. Theorem

**Theorem.** Every admissible same-source package defines a unique map
\[
C\longmapsto
\left(
Z_C,\,
f_C,\,
\widehat\ell_C,\,
\mathcal J_C,\,
\{\mathcal I_{C,\pm}\}
\right)
\]
on its detected projective binary support. The map has the following
properties:

1. \(Z_C\) is a self-adjoint involution.
2. \(f_C\) is the finite spatial projection of the same-action variational
   branch stress difference.
3. \(\widehat\ell_C\) is fixed by the selected external spectral support.
4. \(\mathcal J_C\geq0\) and is an exact finite spectral sum.
5. The instrument is completely positive and normalized.
6. Common scaling of `D_C` cancels exactly.
7. No response normalization, smearing-shape or collapse-rate fit is added.
8. The construction is covariant under simultaneous unitary re-encoding and
   under isometric transport of the spatial spectral package.

### Proof

Injectivity of `D_C` makes \(G_C=D_C^\ast D_C\) strictly positive on the
detected support. Therefore
\[
U_C^\ast U_C
=G_C^{-1/2}D_C^\ast D_CG_C^{-1/2}
=I.
\]
The projective-context assumptions give
\[
Z_C^\ast=Z_C,
\qquad
Z_C^2=(E_{C,+}-E_{C,-})^2=E_{C,+}+E_{C,-}=I.
\]

Metric variation uniquely fixes the stress tensor relative to the selected
matter action and frame. Functional calculus uniquely fixes
\(P_C^{\rm sp}\), \(\widehat G_C\), and \(\widehat\ell_C\) from the selected
external spatial operator and cutoff. Positivity of the reduced inverse gives
\[
\mathcal J_C
=4\pi\|\widehat G_C^{1/2}f_C\|^2\geq0.
\]
Finite rank makes the spectral sum exact; there is no continuum quadrature
remainder inside the selected finite object.

Finally,
\[
\sum_\pm M_{C,\pm}^\ast M_{C,\pm}
=U_C^\ast(\Pi_{C,+}+\Pi_{C,-})U_C
=I.
\]
Thus the operations are completely positive and normalized. Replacing
`D_C` by \(cD_C\) multiplies \(G_C^{-1/2}\) by \(|c|^{-1}\), so a positive
common response scale cancels. Unitary or isometric re-encoding conjugates all
operators and preserves the displayed inner products and traces. This proves
all claims.
